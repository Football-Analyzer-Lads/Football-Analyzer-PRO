FOOTBALL ANALYZER PRO V6.1.2

- Corrected first/second-half shot and SoT modelling: H1/H2 TS and SOT now represent TOTAL home+away volume, while HS/AS and HSOT/ASOT remain team-specific.
- Corrected half-time market signal calculations so both teams' production and concession contribute to total projected volume.
- Start/second-half indices now combine xG/goals with shots and SoT instead of relying on a single metric.
- Kept Over and Under symmetric: the engine does not prefer Under by construction.
- Improved BEST labels for 1H/2H volume markets.
- Expanded match detail popup with first/second-half goals, xG, shots and SoT and clearer home/away start indices.
- Preserved the real-odds requirement for BEST: reference quote >= 1.40 and model probability < 94%.
