Football Analyzer PRO v6.0.3

BEST TIRI/CORNER
- BEST is now restricted to shots, shots on target and corners.
- Goal/1X2/multigol/BTTS markets are no longer eligible for BEST.
- Uses recency-weighted home/away team volume profiles plus opponent conceded volume.
- Generates over/under lines for total, home and away shots, shots on target and corners.
- Targets a playable fair/bookmaker price of at least 1.40 and prefers prices close to 1.40.
- BEST shows the expected mean and the statistical margin versus the selected line.
- Real bookmaker prices are used automatically when the odds provider supplies matching volume-market keys; otherwise the model fair price is used as a temporary gate.
- Early-season venue fallback uses the team's all-venue recency profile when fewer than two venue-specific matches exist.
